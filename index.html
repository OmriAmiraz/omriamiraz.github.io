About

My research is in semantics, typology, historical linguistics, and corpus linguistics. I completed my PhD in Linguistics at the Hebrew University of Jerusalem in 2025. I'm currently a postdoc at Ghent University within the ERC-funded project CAUSALITY, led by Alexandra Simonenko.
